Reversal Patterns

Reversal patterns indicate a change in the prevailing trend. They form after an extended price movement and signal a potential trend reversal.

Head and Shoulders

Bearish Reversal
Three peaks with the middle peak (head) higher than the two outer peaks (shoulders). Forms after an uptrend and signals a bearish reversal.
Trend
Downtrend Reversal
Reliability
Very High
Time Frame
Days to Weeks
Volume
Decreasing

Trading Strategy

Enter short when price breaks below neckline. Target = neckline - (head height). Stop loss above right shoulder.

Inverse Head and Shoulders

Bullish Reversal
Inverted version of head and shoulders. Three troughs with the middle trough (head) lower than the two outer troughs (shoulders). Forms after a downtrend and signals a bullish reversal.
Trend
Uptrend Reversal
Reliability
Very High
Time Frame
Days to Weeks
Volume
Increasing

Trading Strategy

Enter long when price breaks above neckline. Target = neckline + (head depth). Stop loss below left shoulder.

Double Top

Bearish Reversal
Two peaks at approximately the same level with a trough in between. Forms an 'M' shape. Indicates strong resistance and potential downtrend.
Trend
Downtrend Reversal
Reliability
High
Time Frame
Weeks to Months
Volume
Decreasing

Trading Strategy

Sell when price breaks below the support level (trough). Target = support - (peak height - support). Stop loss above peaks.

Double Bottom

Bullish Reversal
Two troughs at approximately the same level with a peak in between. Forms a 'W' shape. Indicates strong support and potential uptrend.
Trend
Uptrend Reversal
Reliability
High
Time Frame
Weeks to Months
Volume
Increasing

Trading Strategy

Buy when price breaks above the resistance level (peak). Target = resistance + (resistance - trough). Stop loss below troughs.

Rounding Bottom (Saucer)

Bullish Reversal
Smooth, rounded bottom formation. Indicates gradual shift from selling to buying pressure. Gentle reversal pattern with high reliability.
Trend
Uptrend Reversal
Reliability
High
Time Frame
Weeks to Months
Volume
Gradually Increasing

Trading Strategy

Buy when price breaks above the resistance line. Volume should increase significantly. Stop loss at the lowest point of the saucer.

Cup and Handle

Bullish Reversal
U-shaped cup followed by a small pullback (handle). Indicates consolidation before a breakout. Very bullish pattern with strong follow-through.
Trend
Uptrend Reversal
Reliability
Very High
Time Frame
Weeks to Months
Volume
Increasing

Trading Strategy

Buy when price breaks above the cup rim. Volume should increase significantly. Target = cup rim + cup depth. Stop loss at cup bottom.

Continuation Patterns

Continuation patterns indicate that the current trend will continue after a period of consolidation. They form during temporary pauses in the trend.

Ascending Triangle

Bullish Continuation
Horizontal resistance with rising support line. Converging pattern indicating buying pressure. Breakout usually occurs upward.
Trend
Bullish Continuation
Reliability
High
Time Frame
Days to Weeks
Volume
Increasing

Trading Strategy

Buy on breakout above resistance. Volume should increase. Target = resistance + triangle height. Stop loss below support line.

Descending Triangle

Bearish Continuation
Horizontal support with declining resistance line. Converging pattern indicating selling pressure. Breakout usually occurs downward.
Trend
Bearish Continuation
Reliability
High
Time Frame
Days to Weeks
Volume
Increasing

Trading Strategy

Sell on breakout below support. Volume should increase. Target = support - triangle height. Stop loss above resistance line.

Symmetrical Triangle

Neutral Continuation
Both support and resistance lines converging at equal angles. Indicates equilibrium between buyers and sellers. Breakout direction depends on volume.
Trend
Neutral Continuation
Reliability
Medium
Time Frame
Days to Weeks
Volume
Decreasing then Increasing

Trading Strategy

Wait for breakout confirmation with volume. Trade in direction of breakout. Target = entry + triangle height. Stop loss at opposite boundary.

Wedge

Continuation/Reversal
Two trend lines converging at an angle. Can be bullish (rising wedge) or bearish (falling wedge). Indicates consolidation before breakout.
Trend

Reliability

Trading Strategy

Trade breakout in direction indicated by wedge. Rising wedge often breaks down; falling wedge often breaks up. Use volume confirmation.

Trading Tips & Best Practices

📊 Confirm with Volume

Always check volume when a pattern breaks out. Increasing volume confirms the breakout; low volume suggests weakness.

🎯 Set Profit Targets

Calculate target prices based on pattern height. For reversal patterns, target = resistance/support ± pattern height.

🛑 Use Stop Losses

Always place stop losses at logical points. For reversals, place stops beyond the opposite boundary of the pattern.

⏰ Timeframe Matters

Patterns on longer timeframes (daily, weekly) are more reliable than shorter ones. Combine multiple timeframes for confirmation.

🔄 Combine Indicators

Use technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages) to confirm pattern signals. Avoid trading patterns in isolation.

📈 Consider Trend

Continuation patterns are more reliable in strong trends. Reversal patterns work best after extended moves.

💡 Wait for Confirmation

Don't trade at the first sign of a pattern. Wait for confirmation signals like breakout with volume or closing above/below key levels.

📝 Keep a Journal

Track your pattern trades. Document which patterns work best for your trading style and market conditions.

Key Takeaway: Chart patterns are powerful tools but not foolproof. Combine them with other technical analysis tools, fundamental analysis, and proper risk management for best results. Always trade with a plan and stick to your rules.
