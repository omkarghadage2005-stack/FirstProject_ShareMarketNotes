Share Market Trading Emotions

Understanding trader psychology

Fear

Fear makes traders sell too early or avoid good opportunities.

Example: You bought a stock at ₹100. It drops to ₹95 and you panic sell, but later it rises to ₹120.

Greed

Greed makes traders hold positions too long expecting higher profits.

Example: Stock goes from ₹100 to ₹150. You expect ₹200 and don’t sell, then price falls to ₹120.

Hope

Hope causes traders to hold losing trades expecting recovery.

Example: Stock falls from ₹200 to ₹150 but you keep holding hoping it returns to ₹200.

Panic

Panic selling happens during sudden market crashes.

Example: Market drops 5% in a day and traders quickly sell without analysis.
